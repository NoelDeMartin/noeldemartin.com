And just like that, VueFes is over!

I really enjoyed this conference, it was very well organized and had a good variety of topics. Unfortunately for me, though, most of the presentations were in Japanese, and the live translation to English... let's just say it wasn't great 😅. So I ended up skipping most of the Japanese presentations, and spent a couple of hours working at the rest area 🤷.

Regardless of that, I wasn't too idle. I managed to speak to a couple of interesting people, and also with some core contributors to some of my favorite projects: Vue, [ElysiaJS](https://elysiajs.com/), [AniList](https://anilist.co/), etc. Still, I can't say I'm satisfied with my networking, because I definitely could have spoken with more people. Sure, the language barrier was a problem, but to be honest I didn't make too much of an effort to speak to more people. This has been a problem at most conferences I attended, specially when there isn't an official "ice-breaker" or networking game.

But, the few people I spoke with, it was fun! Of course, whenever people asked me about my work, I talked about Solid... and very few people knew about it 😅. I'm still amazed that Solid is so obscure given that it is a project by the same person who created the World Wide Web... and that was at a conference full of developers making web applications! So yeah, we Solid developers have a rough road ahead.

On the bright side, and by pure coincidence, a couple of days ago I found out that [Tim's book has recently been released in Japan](https://str.toyokeizai.net/books/9784492445006/)! So I got my hands on one, and had the privilege of seeing how they misspelled my name 🙈:

> 私はテクノロジーを支える人たちに刺激を受けている。彼らの想い、エネルギー、決意こそがより良い未来への原動力だ。**ノエル・デマーティン**はここ5年間、Solidアプリケーションの開発に取り組んでいる。「Solidはウェブにとって（そしてソフトウエア業界全般にとって）最善の選択肢だと今でも思っている。成功する見込みがわずかしかなくても、挑戦しなかったら後悔する」と書いている。

<span class="text-sm">(According to this, my name is "Noel Demartin")</span>

So yeah, all in all, the conference was good, and I'd definitely attend again if I find another excuse to visit Japan at the same time. But I don't think I'd come to Japan just to attend it. Even today, my favorite conference is still [FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org/).
